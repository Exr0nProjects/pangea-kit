# Every instruction needs a control

**What happened.** A caption told the reader to "stop the pulse at the right time" but no control existed to stop it. The reader asked where the control was.

**Lesson.** Any imperative sentence on screen ("drag detuning", "turn the field off", "stop the pulse at 90 degrees") needs a visible control for exactly that action, in the step where the sentence appears, labelled with the action's name and bound to a state key the animation reads. Put the control next to the controls the reader already sees, not at the bottom of a scrolling panel.

**Check.** Read the rendered text of each step and match each instruction to a control label.
